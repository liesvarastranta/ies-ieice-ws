About the Workshop

The Advancing Information and Telecommunication: Bridging Theory and Application workshop is a strategic collaborative initiative between IEICE TC on IT (Japan), IES–PENS, and BRIN (Indonesia). Hosted in Yogyakarta, Indonesia, this event brings together researchers, engineers, and practitioners to strengthen the connection between theoretical innovation and real-world technological solutions in information and telecommunication systems.

The workshop highlights cross-disciplinary topics such as signal processing, networking architectures, IoT and AI-driven systems, 5G/6G communication, and resilient digital infrastructures. Through technical presentations, invited talks, and interactive sessions, participants will explore how fundamental theories can lead to impactful implementations across industries and research communities.

Student participation is strongly encouraged as part of our commitment to cultivating the next generation of innovators. Students are invited to present their research, share prototypes, and engage in feedback sessions designed to promote international mentorship and collaboration between Japan and Indonesia.

Join us in Yogyakarta to bridge theory and application—where ideas evolve into innovation, and future researchers find their voice.

Free Cross-Attendance

Participation in both events is free. IEICE participants may attend the IES parallel sessions, and IES participants may attend the IEICE keynote sessions without an additional fee. In a typical conference, access to parallel sessions often requires a separate payment, but that restriction does not apply here.

IEICE Workshop Track

Advantages

  • ✓ Lower barrier to entry: Register first, then prepare the full paper after screening
  • ✓ Fast feedback: Know if your registration is approved within 10 days
  • ✓ Later deadline: More time to prepare the full manuscript
  • ✓ Affordable: Low registration fee (3,000 JPY)
  • ✓ International visibility: IEICE collaboration with Japan
  • ✓ Ideal for work-in-progress: Present early-stage research

Remarks

  • ✗ Two-stage workflow: Register first, then submit the full paper in the IEICE submission system
  • ✗ Less rigorous review: No formal peer review process
  • ✗ Technical Report format: Not as prestigious as IEEE proceedings
  • ✗ Narrow scope: Must fit IT/telecommunications topics
  • ✗ Separate payment portal: Payment handled in the IEICE submission system

IES Track

Advantages

  • ✓ Higher prestige: IEEE-standard proceedings with formal peer review
  • ✓ Simpler process: Submission via familiar EDAS platform
  • ✓ Rigorous feedback: Constructive reviewer comments improve your work
  • ✓ Broader topics: Welcome all electronics and engineering fields
  • ✓ Better for CVs: IEEE-compatible publication carries more weight
  • ✓ Clear workflow: Established EDAS system, no dual submission

Remarks

  • ✗ Earlier deadline: Full paper due May 4
  • ✗ Higher risk: Write full paper before knowing if it will be accepted
  • ✗ More competitive: Rigorous review may lead to rejection
  • ✗ Higher cost: Registration fees $150-$350 USD
  • ✗ Slower feedback: Review process takes longer than 10 days

Workshop Speakers

Prof. Jun Muramatsu

Waseda University, Japan, IEICE TC on IT

Presentation Topic: TBA

Dr. Jun Muramatsu received his B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. degrees in Mathematics from Nagoya University, Japan, in 1990, 1992, and 1998, respectively. From 1992 to 2025, he was with the Nippon Telegraph and Telephone Corporation (NTT), where he served as a Research Scientist at NTT Communication Science Laboratories. He also held an international research appointment as a visiting researcher at ETH Zürich, Switzerland (2007–2008). In 2025, he joined the Faculty of Science and Engineering at Waseda University, Japan, as a Professor. His research interests span the foundations of information theory, Shannon theory, source and channel coding, random number generation, and information-theoretic security.

Prof. I Gede Puja Astawa

Politeknik Elektronika Negeri Surabaya (PENS), Indonesia

Presentation Topic: TBA

Prof. I Gede Puja Astawa received his B.S. and M.S. degrees from Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember Surabaya (ITS), Indonesia in 1993 and 2006,respectively. He received his Ph.D. degree from the Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST), Japan, in 2012. He is currently with the Politeknik Elektronika Negeri Surabaya (PENS), where he serves as a Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering. His research interests in wireless communications, signal processing include OFDM and MIMO systems, physical-layer security, peak-to-average power ratio (PAPR) reduction techniques, and real-time experimental validation using SDR platforms.

Dr. Budiman Putra Asmaur Rohman

Badan Riset dan Inovasi Nasional (BRIN), Indonesia
